  question: Which one of the statements is not valid for aerosols
(NEET 2017)
  answer: They cause increased agricultural productivity
  explanation: Aerosol are harmful environmental pollutants which have negative effects on Agriculture. Aerosol is the colloid of fine solid particles in the air such as fog. It is type of pollutant that hinders the sunlight to reach the plants and thus, plants are not able to trap light for photosynthesis. In the absence of photosynthesis the agricultural productivity get decreased.

The following are the environmental, agricultural and health issues caused by aerosols:
- They are harmful to human health
- They alter rainfall and monsoon patterns
- They have a negative impact on agricultural land causing a decrease in agricultural productivity.
